Blobs drive on the floor, walls and ramps, flip, and fly on boost. Hit the ball with speed to hit it hard. Big pads refill boost, small ones top it up. Ram an opponent at supersonic speed (white streaks behind you) and you splat them; they come back near their end 3 seconds later. In Sinkhole the goals are holes in the floor: the jelly over a hole swallows the ball but throws blobs back up, so you can't park on it, and a bounce gives you your flip back. In Spikes the ball sticks to spiky blobs; bump the carrier to knock it loose.
